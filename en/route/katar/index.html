Road trip

The Cathar loop

Foix → Carcassonne via the castles · 230 km

Distance
230 km

Two or three days among the Cathar castles, based in Bélesta or Quillan. Montségur and Peyrepertuse require an uphill walk – go in the mornings before the heat.
