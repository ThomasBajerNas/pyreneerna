Pamplona

Bull running if you dare

From the 2014 guide – ThomasBull running if you dare.

The capital of Navarre. San Fermín with the running of the bulls (encierro) takes place on 6–14 July every year. The rest of the year it's a quiet city with well-preserved city walls and the Hemingway café, Café Iruña, on the Plaza del Castillo.
