Montcalm

The rounded giant of Ariège, 3 077 m

The easternmost 3,000-metre peak lies entirely in France, at the head of the Vicdessos valley above Auzat. A broad, rounded summit with no difficult sections – together with Pica d'Estats a classic two-day trip from Refuge du Pinet.

Montcalm is the easternmost peak over 3 000 metres in the Pyrenees and the highest lying entirely in Ariège.

How to get up – the normal route step by step

  1. Day 1: from L'Artigue up to Refuge du Pinet, approx. 3 hours.
  2. Day 2: via Étang de Montcalm (approx. 2 560 m) and up the broad ridge to the summit.
  3. Most people continue to Pica d'Estats and do a loop.
SafetyA long day – start early. In mist the broad summit is hard to navigate.
