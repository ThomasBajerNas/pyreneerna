La Molina

Cerdanya (Alp), Girona, Catalonia

La Molina is regarded as Spain's oldest ski resort with a lift. The first commercial lift was built in 1943. The resort lies in the Cerdanya and is run by FGC. Via the gondola over Tosa d'Alp it is linked to Masella, and together they form the Alp 2500 ski area. La Molina has hosted the Alpine Ski World Cup (2008) and the Snowboard World Championships (2011). It is also one of the few resorts in the Pyrenees you can reach by train from Barcelona.

Getting here

Flights
Barcelona (BCN) approx. 160 km, approx. 2 h · Girona (GRO) approx. 150 km, approx. 2 h · Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell (LEU) approx. 65 km, approx. 1 h · Perpignan (PGF) approx. 140 km, approx. 2 h 15 min · Toulouse (TLS) approx. 200 km, approx. 2 h 45 min
Train
La Molina station (Rodalies R3 Barcelona–Vic–Ripoll–Puigcerdà), shuttle bus up to the lifts
Bus
Shuttle bus from the railway station and between the parts of the resort. Ski buses from the Cerdanya during the season.
Car
C-17/N-152 via Ripoll and the Collada de Toses, or the C-16 through the Túnel del Cadí (toll) to Alp and the GI-400 up to La Molina. There are several car parks, including at Pista Llarga, the Cadí-Moixeró gondola and Alabaus. Chains needed in snow.
