BTT Cerdanya

Cerdanya, Girona/Lleida (Catalonia) – borders the French Cerdagne

Trails
approx. 1,400 km of waymarked routes and 90 loops in the MTB limitless guide (Cerdanya, Capcir and Pyrénées-Catalanes); 25 loops are presented on cerdanya.org
Disciplines
XC, all mountain, singletrack, e-MTB
Season
approx. May–October
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels
Hire
several bike shops in Puigcerdà, Bellver and Alp; price unknown

Cerdanya is a wide, sunny high plateau at approx. 1,100–1,200 m surrounded by mountains – Europe's largest east–west valley, here in the Pyrenees. The loops start from Puigcerdà, Llívia, Bellver, Alp and La Molina and cross freely into the French Cerdagne. The abundant sunshine and gentle gradients make the area good for families, but there are also long singletracks for more experienced riders.

Highlights

  • Cross-border network with France
  • Sunny high plateau
  • Combine with La Molina or Les Angles
