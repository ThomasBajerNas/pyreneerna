Espace nordique d'Issarbe

Vallée de Barétous, Pyrénées-Atlantiques

Issarbe is a small, quiet cross-country area in beech forest and open pastures above the Barétous valley. The trails lie at about 1,450 m with views of Pic d'Anie and the limestone mountains around La Pierre Saint-Martin. It is cheap, friendly and rarely crowded. A small refuge with 15 beds lies in the middle of the area.

Getting here

Flights
Pau-Pyrénées (PUF) approx. 85 km, approx. 1 h 30 min · Biarritz (BIQ) approx. 140 km, approx. 2 h · Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 120 km, approx. 1 h 50 min
Train
Oloron-Sainte-Marie (TER from Pau), approx. 40 km – by car from there
Car
From Oloron-Sainte-Marie via Aramits and Lanne-en-Barétous (D919), with the last 17–18 km uphill on a mountain road to the chalet. Free parking. Carry snow chains or have winter tyres.
