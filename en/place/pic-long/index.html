Pic Long

The highest peak entirely in France, 3 192 m

In the Néouvielle area, above the Cap de Long lake, stands the highest peak that is entirely French. It has no easy walking route and is a goal for alpinists.

First ascent
1890 – Henri Brulle with the guides Célestin Passet and François Bernat-Salles

Henri Brulle and Célestin Passet were the great climbing partnership of the golden age of the Pyrenees in the late 19th century – the same year they also climbed the Couloir de Gaube on Vignemale.

How to get up – the normal route step by step

  1. Pic Long has no easy walking route: every way up is an alpine route with steep couloirs, abseils and glacier remnants.
  2. Go with a mountain guide if you have no alpine experience. The mountain guides in the Hautes-Pyrénées organise trips.
SafetyFor alpinists. If you want to experience Néouvielle without a rope, walk around the lakes at Lac d'Aumar.
