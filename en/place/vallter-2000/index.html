Vallter 2000

Ripollès (Setcases), Girona, Catalonia

Vallter 2000 is the easternmost alpine resort in the Pyrenees. It lies in a high cirque at the source of the river Ter above the village of Setcases. The base is at 2,000 m, and on a clear day you can see all the way to the Mediterranean. The resort is small and run by FGC, with easy and intermediate pistes. It is popular for day trips from Girona and Barcelona.

Girona (GRO) approx. 110 km, approx. 1 h 45 min · Barcelona (BCN) approx. 160 km, approx. 2 h 15 min · Perpignan (PGF) approx. 115 km, approx. 2 h via the Col d'Ares

Ripoll (Rodalies R3) approx. 45 km, then bus or car
Bus
Regional buses to Camprodon. Shuttle bus and ski bus from the car parks to the resort.
Car
C-38 via Camprodon to Setcases and on along the winding GIV-5264 up to the resort (approx. 12 km). Free parking at the resort. Chains are often needed.
