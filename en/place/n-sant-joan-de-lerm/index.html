Sant Joan de l'Erm

Alt Urgell (Catalonia)

Sant Joan de l'Erm is Catalonia's largest cross-country resort, opened as early as 1970. The trails run through forests of fir, birch and mountain pine in the Parc Natural de l'Alt Pirineu. The base is the Refugi de la Basseta at 1,720 m. Many loops are easy, but there are also longer and tougher routes.
