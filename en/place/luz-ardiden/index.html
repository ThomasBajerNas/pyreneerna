Luz-Ardiden lies high above Luz-Saint-Sauveur in three sectors, Aulian, Béderet and Caperette-Cloze, and has one of the most varied areas in the Pyrenees for experienced skiers. The summit at 2,500 m gives views of Vignemale and the Gavarnie massif. Around 60% of the pistes are red or black, and the Bernazaou valley is a classic freeride and ski-touring area. The resort opened in 1975 and is a famous Tour de France climb with nine stage finishes, most recently Tadej Pogačar in 2021.

Highlights

  • Belle Bleue, a blue piste approx. 5 km long
  • Freeride in Caperette-Cloze and the descent towards Bernazaou
  • Tour de France finish nine times (1985–2021)
  • Free shuttle from Luz-Saint-Sauveur

Getting here

Flights
Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 55 km, approx. 1 h · Pau (PUF) approx. 95 km, approx. 1 h 35 min · Toulouse (TLS) approx. 215 km, approx. 2 h 30 min
Train
Lourdes (TGV/TER), then liO bus to Luz-Saint-Sauveur and the free shuttle up
Bus
liO buses Lourdes–Luz-Saint-Sauveur; free daily shuttle Luz–Luz-Ardiden (Aulian) in winter; ski buses from Tarbes airport, Lourdes station, Pau and Toulouse at weekends and during school holidays
Car
A64 exit Tarbes Ouest/Lourdes, N21 to Pierrefitte-Nestalas and the D921 to Luz-Saint-Sauveur, then 12 km of mountain road up. Winter tyres or chains/snow socks compulsory 1 Nov–31 March. Free parking at Aulian and Béderet
