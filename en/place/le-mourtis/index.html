Le Mourtis

Boutx, Haut-Comminges, Haute-Garonne

Le Mourtis is a small, quiet forest resort on a grassy plateau above Saint-Béat, and the Pyrenean resort closest to Toulouse. The pistes wind between spruces and pines, which gives good visibility even on a grey day. The first chairlift opened in 1965. Here you stay in chalets in the middle of the forest, and the atmosphere is relaxed and friendly.

Highlights

  • The closest of all Pyrenean resorts to Toulouse (approx. 125 km)
  • Sheltered forest pistes – good when it is windy or visibility is poor
  • Snowshoeing, sledging slope and ski tours from the resort

Getting here

Flights
Toulouse-Blagnac (TLS) approx. 135 km, approx. 1 h 35 min · Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 105 km, approx. 1 h 20 min
Train
liO train Toulouse–Montréjeau–Marignac-Saint-Béat. The free Vali bus runs from there to the resort via the Col de Menté (weekends and school holidays). Skirail package €39.50 (2025/26) with train, bus and day pass.
Bus
Free Vali ski bus from Marignac-Saint-Béat station via Cierp-Gaud, Saint-Béat and Boutx to the Artigue, Front de neige and Courraou sectors
Car
A64 exit Saint-Gaudens/Montréjeau, towards Saint-Béat, then the D44 over the Col de Menté. Winter tyres or chains needed. Parking at the resort's sectors.
