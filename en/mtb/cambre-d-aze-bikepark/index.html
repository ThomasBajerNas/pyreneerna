Cambre d'Aze Enduro Park

Eyne and Saint-Pierre-dels-Forcats, Cerdagne, Pyrénées-Orientales

Trails
approx. 10 trails in 2026 (6 the year before), incl. the long green Tommie Pittie, the blue Bluebird and two new blacks
Lifts
the El Mouli chairlift
Disciplines
enduro, flow
Season
until 30 August 2026, 9.30–18.00
Lift pass
adult day pass €25, reduced €21.50, single ride €13.50, season €170 (2026)
Level
all levels
Hire
Mondraker bikes from €45

Cambre d'Aze focuses on enduro and has quickly grown into a popular park in the Cerdagne. All trails start from the El Mouli chairlift and run through pine forest between approx. 1,600 and 2,000 m. The range grew significantly in 2026 with new trails at every level. The atmosphere is friendly, with social rides and the Catalan-Duro race.

Highlights

  • Catalan-Duro 29–30 August 2026
  • Endugrill pack with pass and lunch, €35
  • Rapidly growing trail range
