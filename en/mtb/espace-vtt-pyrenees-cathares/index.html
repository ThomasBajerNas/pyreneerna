Espace VTT-FFC Pyrénées Cathares

Pays d'Olmes (Lavelanet, Montségur), Ariège

Disciplines
XC, all mountain, e-MTB
Season
approx. April–November
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels

The area around Lavelanet lies at the foot of the Cathar castle of Montségur and combines cycling with history. The loops run through forest and hilly foothills with views of the castle and the Plantaurel. The Grande Traversée VTT passes Montségur, so the area works well as a stopover on a longer trip.

Highlights

  • Views of the Cathar castle of Montségur
  • Part of the Grande Traversée VTT
  • Quiet, hilly foothills
