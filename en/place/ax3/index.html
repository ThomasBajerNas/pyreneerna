Ax 3 Domaines is the largest resort in Ariège, with three linked areas: Bonascre, Saquet and Campels. The summit is at 2,400 m and the longest run is about 11 km, all the way down towards Ax-les-Thermes. The resort opened in 1955 and was called Ax-Bonascre until 2001. The gondola from the spa town of Ax-les-Thermes means you can get here by train from Toulouse. The Plateau de Bonascre has been a Tour de France finish several times.

Highlights

  • Gondola straight from Ax-les-Thermes – close to the railway station
  • Long runs through the forest down to Bonascre, the longest approx. 11 km
  • Views from Campels over Haute-Ariège and towards Andorra
  • End the day in the thermal baths of Ax-les-Thermes

Getting here

Flights
Toulouse-Blagnac (TLS) approx. 140 km, approx. 1 h 45 min · Carcassonne (CCF) approx. 110 km, approx. 1 h 50 min · Perpignan (PGF) approx. 145 km, approx. 2 h 30 min
Train
Ax-les-Thermes station on the Toulouse–Latour-de-Carol line (liO train, approx. 1 h 45 min from Toulouse). The Télécabine du Baou gondola runs from the town up to Bonascre. Skirail packages with train and day pass are available from Toulouse.
Bus
Winter bus between Ax-les-Thermes and Savignac-les-Ormeaux
Car
N20 Toulouse–Foix–Ax-les-Thermes, then the D82 approx. 8 km up to the Plateau de Bonascre. Large car parks at Bonascre. Winter tyres or chains needed on the road up.
