Port Ainé

Rialp, Pallars Sobirà, Lleida, Catalonia

Port Ainé opened in 1986 and lies on a sunny ridge above Rialp and Sort in Pallars Sobirà. The resort is run by FGC and has its own hotel at 2,000 m. The pistes run through forest and open terrain up to Pic de l'Orri (2,440 m), with wide views over the Noguera Pallaresa valley. There are also 37 km of off-piste routes.
