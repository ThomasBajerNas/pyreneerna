Balneario de Panticosa

A high-alpine spa on the Spanish side

A spa at 1 640 metres, in a lake cirque surrounded by three-thousand-metre peaks. Reached via the Col du Pourtalet from Laruns.

Panticosa is the smaller and quieter part of Formigal-Panticosa. It lies by the old village of Panticosa, from where a gondola runs up to the Petrosos plateau. The area is not linked to Formigal by lift, but the same lift pass is valid at both. From the village at 1,145 m you get Aramón's biggest vertical drop. The slopes are mostly red and blue, in a sheltered valley with views over the Valle de Tena. The village is a pleasant alternative to Formigal's modern development, and the Balneario de Panticosa spa lies a little further up the valley.

Highlights

  • Gondola straight from Panticosa village
  • Quieter slopes than at Formigal
  • Petrosos Park and family-friendly pistes
  • Close to the Balneario de Panticosa with its hot springs
