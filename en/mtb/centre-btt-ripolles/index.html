Centre BTT El Ripollès

Ripollès, Girona (Catalonia)

Trails

Disciplines
XC, all mountain, e-MTB
Season
April–November; high routes June–October
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels

The Ripollès centre is in Sant Joan de les Abadesses, on the old Ruta del Ferro railway, which is now a gravel cycle path. From here you can reach quiet valleys, monastery villages and higher routes towards the Vall de Camprodon and Vall de Núria. The Pirinexus loop passes through the town.

Highlights

  • Ruta del Ferro (old railway)
  • Medieval monasteries and bridges
  • Close to Pirinexus and Camprodon
