Gourette lies in a dramatic valley below the Pic du Ger, close to the Col d'Aubisque. All trails start at the top of the Bézou gondola and give approx. 300 m of descent per run. There is a clear progression from green to black and a dedicated park for children. Two long rides also run all the way from the Aubisque down to Laruns and Louvie-Soubiron.

Highlights

  • Kids Park for the youngest
  • Long descents from the Col d'Aubisque to Laruns
  • Social rides every weekend in July–August

Getting here

Getting here
Pau (PUF) approx. 1 h 05 min, Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 1 h 20 min. Train to Pau, then bus via Laruns.
Accommodation
Gourette, Eaux-Bonnes or Laruns.
TipsThere is a repair station at the top of the Bézou and water at Le Cairn. Combine it with a ride over the Aubisque.
