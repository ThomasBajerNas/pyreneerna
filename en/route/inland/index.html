Road trip

The inland road Biarritz → Perpignan

The E80 along the mountains, then through Ariège and Cathar country · 520 km

Distance
520 km

From the 2014 guide: start on the E80 on the French side. Just east of Saint-Gaudens you can take the inland route and come out at Perpignan. There's plenty to see all the way. At Luchon you can instead head south into Spain via Vielha towards Barcelona, and from Foix you can go straight down via Ax towards Andorra and on to Barcelona.
