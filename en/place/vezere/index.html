The Vézère Valley and Lascaux

The valley of prehistory in the Dordogne

Les Eyzies with the National Museum of Prehistory and the Font-de-Gaume cave, and 25 km upstream Montignac with Lascaux IV.

UNESCO World Heritage

Prehistoric Sites and Decorated Caves of the Vézère Valley

World Heritage since
1979

147 prehistoric sites and 25 decorated caves, among them Lascaux with its 17,000-year-old paintings of horses, aurochs and deer. Les Eyzies is known as the ”capital of prehistory”.

Visit: The original Lascaux cave has been closed since 1963. The complete replica Lascaux IV at Montignac is very well done. Font-de-Gaume in Les Eyzies has genuine paintings but few places – book ahead.
