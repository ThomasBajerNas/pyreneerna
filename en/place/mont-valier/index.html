Mont Valier

The Couserans' legendary mountain

The 2 838-metre summit can be seen from large parts of Ariège, and on a clear day all the way from Toulouse. The mountain is named after Saint Valier, the first bishop of the Couserans, and there are many legends about it. The ascent starts from Bordes-sur-Lez via the Refuge des Estagnous and normally takes two days – for experienced hikers. The view of the Valier is best from the Col de Péguère, the Col de Port and the Col d'Agnes, especially in the evening light.

The mountain is named after the first bishop of Couserans. During the Second World War, escape routes crossed the mountains here into Spain.

How to get up – the normal route step by step

  1. Day 1: from Pla de la Lau up to Refuge des Estagnous.
  2. Day 2: over Col Faustin and up the south side to the summit.
  3. Descend the same way to Pla de la Lau.
SafetyVery long: 1 900 metres of ascent. Only for the very fit.
