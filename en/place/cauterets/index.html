Cauterets – Bains du Rocher

Thermal baths in a belle époque town

The Bains du Rocher has indoor and outdoor pools with sulphurous mountain water. Cauterets is also the gateway to the Pont d'Espagne.

Cauterets is an elegant Belle Époque spa town, and the skiing takes place in the Cirque du Lys, a high mountain bowl reached by gondola straight from the village in under 12 minutes. Its position often brings a lot of snow; in the winter of 2013 more than 5 m was measured. From the summits, the Crêtes du Lys at about 2,400 m, you can see far across the western Pyrenees. The ski club was founded in 1907, and as early as 1910 the French championships were held here. Combine it with the thermal baths and cross-country skiing at Pont d'Espagne.

Highlights

  • Gondola from the village up to the Cirque du Lys (1,850 m)
  • Snowy mountain bowl with wide views
  • Cross-country skiing at Pont d'Espagne in the national park
  • Thermal baths in the village after skiing

Bus
liO buses Lourdes–Cauterets; the resort's ski buses from Bordeaux, Lourdes, Tarbes airport, San Sebastián and Dax; shuttle from Cauterets to Pont d'Espagne
Car
A64 exit Soumoulou or Tarbes Ouest, then the N21 towards Argelès-Gazost and the D920 up to Cauterets. Winter tyres or chains/snow socks compulsory 1 Nov–31 March. Park at Courbet by the gondola's valley station; paid car parks in the centre
