Chemin des Cascades

The waterfalls between Cauterets and Pont d'Espagne

The path from La Raillère up to the Pont d'Espagne follows the river past the Lutour, Cerisey, Pas de l'Ours and Boussès falls. About two hours up, and if you don't want to walk back you can take the bus down.
