Espace VTT-FFC Couserans Pyrénées

Couserans (Saint-Girons), Ariège

Trails
24 loops, over 475 km: 4 green, 7 blue, 7 red, 5 black; plus 4 enduro trails, 2 e-MTB loops and a 117 km Tour des portes du Couserans
Disciplines
XC, enduro, e-MTB, multi-day
Season
approx. April–November; high parts June–October
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels

The Couserans is the western part of Ariège – a green, sparsely populated landscape with 18 valleys, beech forests and small villages. The FFC area starts from Saint-Girons and has an unusually large number of black loops as well as its own enduro trails, e.g. the Enduro du Sourroque. A longer 117 km loop can be done over two to three days.

Highlights

  • Enduro du Sourroque
  • Tour des portes du Couserans (117 km)
  • Saint-Lizier – finish of the Grande Traversée VTT
