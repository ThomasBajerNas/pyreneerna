Plateau de Beille

Haute-Ariège, Ariège

Plateau de Beille is the highest large cross-country area in the Pyrenees, at 1,800–2,000 m above Les Cabannes. The trails run across a broad high plateau with 360-degree views of the Ariège Pyrenees. The snow is usually reliable throughout the season. The site is also a famous Tour de France finish, where Pantani and Contador, among others, have won stages.
