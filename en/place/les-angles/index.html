Les Angles

Finish town 6 July 2026

A ski and summer resort in the Capcir at about 1 600 metres. The Parc animalier des Angles wildlife park has bears, wolves and lynx.

Les Angles is a lively ski village on the Capcir plateau with pistes that start in the middle of the village and reach up to 2,400 m. The ski area has five sectors: Village and Pla del Mir for beginners, Plateau de Bigorre in the middle, Balcère and the steep Jassettes with the Mur Mermet. From the top you look out over the lakes of the Capcir and towards Carlit. The Lake Park snowpark opened in 1991 as the first in the Pyrenees.

Highlights

  • Four freeride areas, including Costa Verde and Roc de Péborny
  • Free night skiing 17–19 during school holidays
  • The Lou Bac Mountain toboggan run and the Angléo swimming baths
  • The Les Pèlerins gondola from the village up to the Plateau de Bigorre (2,100 m)

Train
Train jaune to Mont-Louis-La Cabanasse (approx. 15 km), then bus or taxi
Bus
Free shuttles within the resort (including to Pla del Mir); regional liO buses from Perpignan via Mont-Louis
Car
From Perpignan take the N116 to Mont-Louis, then the D118 to Les Angles. From Carcassonne the D118 via Quillan and Axat. Free underground car park in the village. Winter tyres or chains needed.
