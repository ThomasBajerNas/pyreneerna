Road trip

Béziers and the Canal du Midi

A day along the canal · 40 km

Distance
40 km

Start at the Fonserannes locks in the morning when the boats are going through, walk along the canal over the Pont-canal de l'Orb and up to the cathedral. Afternoon at the Oppidum d'Ensérune with views over Montady and the Malpas tunnel, evening swim at Valras-Plage.
