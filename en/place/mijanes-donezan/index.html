Mijanès-Donezan

Donezan, Ariège

Mijanès-Donezan lies in the remote Donezan area, wedged between Ariège, Aude and the Capcir. The resort was created in 1961 and is small and good value, with both alpine and cross-country skiing from the same base. The pistes run through forest with wide views over the Donezan mountains. People come here for the peace and quiet, not for a big ski area.

The Donezan is known as 'Ariège-Québec': a remote, wooded and snowy corner in the far east of Ariège. The trails run through deep spruce forest at 1,530–1,730 m. It is quiet here and there are few people. A good choice for anyone who wants a taste of the wilderness.

Highlights

  • A wilderness feel in deep forest
  • Trails to the Cabane de la Mire
  • Cheap trail pass
  • Snowy location
