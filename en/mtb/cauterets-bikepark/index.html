Bike Park Cauterets (Cirque du Lys)

Vallée de Cauterets, Hautes-Pyrénées

bike, protection and helmet hire at partner shops in the village, not at the bike park

Cauterets offers one of the longest descents in the Pyrenees: from the ridge above the Cirque du Lys at approx. 2,400 m down to the village at approx. 900 m. That is approx. 1,500 m of descent and more than 45 minutes of riding. The trail has berms, step-ups and road gaps. The views over the cirque and towards the Vignemale massif are magnificent.

Highlights

  • Approx. 1,500 m of descent down to the village
  • Gondola straight from Cauterets
  • Enduro trail 108V and FFC route no. 3 back to the village

Getting here

Getting here
Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 50 min, Pau (PUF) approx. 1 h 20 min. Train to Lourdes and bus to Cauterets.
Accommodation
The village of Cauterets is the best base; Argelès-Gazost also works.
TipsHire your gear before taking the gondola. Helmets are compulsory and full protection is recommended. Park at the old railway station.
