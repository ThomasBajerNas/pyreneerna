Lac d'Oô

A mountain lake with a waterfall near Luchon

From the Granges d'Astau it's about an hour's walk up to the lake, where the Cascade d'Oô plunges about 275 metres at the far end. Inn by the lake. If you have more energy, the path continues up to the Lac d'Espingo.
