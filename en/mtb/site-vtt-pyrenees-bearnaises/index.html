Site VTT-FFC des Pyrénées Béarnaises

Haut-Béarn (Oloron-Sainte-Marie), Pyrénées-Atlantiques

Disciplines
XC, all mountain
Season
almost all year round in the foothills; higher up June–October
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels

The FFC area around Oloron-Sainte-Marie covers the foothills at the mouths of the Aspe and Ossau valleys. The loops run through hilly farmland, along mountain rivers and through forest – e.g. Bords du Gave, 34 km with fine singletrack along the Gave d'Ossau. The Grande Traversée VTT des Pyrénées continues from here towards Soule in the Basque Country.

Highlights

  • Bords du Gave (34 km, red)
  • Gateway to the Aspe and Ossau valleys
  • Linked to the Grande Traversée VTT towards the Basque Country
