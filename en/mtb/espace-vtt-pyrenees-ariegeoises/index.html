Espace VTT-FFC des Pyrénées Ariégeoises

Haute-Ariège (Ax-les-Thermes), Ariège

Trails
21 loops, approx. 231 km: 3 green, 10 blue, 5 red, 3 black; plus 4 freeride trails in the Ax 3 Domaines bike park
Disciplines
XC, all mountain, singletrack, e-MTB
Season
approx. May–October
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels

The spa town of Ax-les-Thermes, with its hot springs, is the base for this FFC area in the upper Ariège valley. The loops climb from the valley floor up to villages and summer pastures, offering both climbs and fine singletrack, e.g. 21 km around the Rocher de Miglos. The Grande Traversée VTT Ariège Pyrénées also starts here.

Highlights

  • Hot baths in Ax-les-Thermes after the ride
  • Start of the Grande Traversée VTT
  • Close to the Ax 3 Domaines bike park
