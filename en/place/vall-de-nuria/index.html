Vall de Núria

Ripollès (Queralbs), Girona, Catalonia

Vall de Núria is a small resort in a high valley with no road access. You can only get there on the Cremallera de Núria rack railway from Ribes de Freser or Queralbs, which climbs about 1,000 vertical metres. The valley has a pilgrimage sanctuary, a hotel and a lake, surrounded by peaks of almost 3,000 m. The pistes are few and easy, and suit families with children and beginners. FGC has been granted a new concession running until 2055.

Getting here

Flights
Barcelona (BCN) approx. 125 km to Ribes de Freser, approx. 1 h 45 min · Girona (GRO) approx. 110 km, approx. 1 h 30 min · Perpignan (PGF) approx. 140 km, approx. 2 h
Train
Rodalies R3 to Ribes de Freser, then the rack railway from Ribes-Enllaç (approx. 40 min)
Car
C-17 and N-260 via Ripoll to Ribes de Freser. Park at the rack railway in Ribes or Queralbs. There is no road to Núria.
