Ordino Arcalís lies at the top of the Ordino valley in north-west Andorra. It is a quieter and wilder area than Grandvalira. The resort was founded in 1983 by the people of Ordino and has no village at the slopes, just lifts, restaurants and large car parks at El Hortell and La Coma. The snow often keeps well here thanks to the altitude. Ordino Arcalís is one of Europe's best-known freeride resorts and a stop on the Freeride World Tour. From the Tristaina gondola you reach the Mirador Solar de Tristaina viewpoint (2,701 m).

Highlights

  • Freeride World Tour and 21 freeride areas with 120 lines
  • The Mirador Solar de Tristaina viewing platform above the Tristaina lakes
  • The Megaverda Kids Forest trail for children (8.5 km)
  • Quieter slopes and short lift queues

Getting here

Flights
Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell (LEU) approx. 35 km, approx. 50 min · Barcelona (BCN) approx. 215 km, approx. 3 h 15 min · Toulouse (TLS) approx. 215 km, approx. 3 h 15 min
Bus
Andorra's scheduled buses run from Andorra la Vella to Ordino and El Serrat. Check whether there is a ski bus up to Arcalís for the season.
Car
CG-3 from Andorra la Vella through La Massana and Ordino, then the Carretera d'Arcalís up to El Hortell (La Basera) and La Coma, approx. 17 km from Ordino. Free parking for approx. 1,500 cars. Chains are often needed, and the road may be closed temporarily when there is avalanche danger.

Accommodation

Accommodation
No accommodation at the slopes. Accommodation is available in the villages further down the valley.
Places to stay nearby
El Serrat (approx. 10 min), Ordino and La Cortinada (approx. 20 min), La Massana (approx. 25 min), Andorra la Vella (approx. 30 min)
TipsArrive early after a snowfall, as the freeride zones often open quickly. The Nord Pass and the Andorra Pass are valid both here and at Pal Arinsal. Bring a packed lunch or eat at La Coma, as there is less choice on the mountain than in Grandvalira.
