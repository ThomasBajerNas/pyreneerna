Balaïtous

The westernmost 3,000-metre peak, 3 144 m

A wild granite massif on the border between Béarn and Aragon, with the Las Néous glacier on the French side. From the Spanish side you start from Sallent de Gállego and the La Sarra dam.

Balaïtous is the westernmost peak over 3 000 metres. It is often called the wildest in the Pyrenees: a long approach, granite and few people.

How to get up – the normal route step by step

  1. Day 1: from La Sarra along the valley and the lake up to Refugio de Respomuso, 2–3 hours.
  2. Day 2: past the lakes up towards the south side. The Gran Diagonal is a long, slanting ledge across the south face, with easy climbing (I).
  3. From the ledge you climb through boulders to the summit.
  4. Descend the same way.
SafetyA helmet is needed – rockfall from parties above. The route via Brecha Latour is harder (PD, II).

Other routes up

From France you start from Refuge de Larribet and cross the Las Néous glacier.
