Espace nordique de l'Étang de Lers

Couserans/Vicdessos, Ariège

The Étang de Lers is a mountain lake at about 1,270 m by the Col de Lers, between Massat and Vicdessos. The trails run around the lake and up towards the pass through open countryside with pastures and forest. It is a small, beautiful and quiet area, well known to cyclists from the Tour de France.
