Espace nordique du Val d'Azun (Soulor–Couraduque)

Val d'Azun, Hautes-Pyrénées

Val d'Azun is the largest cross-country area in the Hautes-Pyrénées, with around 90 km of trails. The trails link two legendary cycling passes, the Col de Couraduque and the Col du Soulor, with wide views over the Val d'Azun. There is a Nordic Park with a moving carpet and biathlon ranges for beginners and children. It is also one of the few places with adapted equipment for people with reduced mobility.

Highlights

  • The longest trail network in the Pyrenees west of Ariège
  • Two entry points: Couraduque and Soulor
  • Biathlon for beginners
  • Col du Soulor – famous from the Tour de France
