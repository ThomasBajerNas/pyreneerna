Espace nordique de Nistos (Cap Nestès)

Vallée de Nistos, Hautes-Pyrénées

Nistos is a small family resort between the Vallée d'Aure and the Val d'Aran, at 1,600–1,800 m. The trails are mostly easy and run through forest and over open ridges with views of the high peaks. The Le Cap Nestès restaurant and a gîte are at the trailhead. The setting is secluded and quiet.

Getting here

Flights

Lannemezan or Montréjeau (TER Toulouse–Tarbes), by car from there
Car
From the A64 (Lannemezan exit) via Nestier/Nistos up the valley. Narrow mountain road on the last stretch – chains or winter tyres.
