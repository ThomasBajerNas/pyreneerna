Saint-Lary Bike Park (Pla d'Adet)

Vallée d'Aure, Hautes-Pyrénées

Trails
10 trails plus a beginners' zone, from green to black, incl. Family Rider, Easy Rider, Woodstock (4.4 km), K-Bourre and Razorback (over 40 km of waymarked trails according to the resort)
Lifts
the Bouleaux chairlift (with bike carriers); the cable car from the village is included in the more expensive pass
Disciplines
downhill, freeride, enduro, beginners' zone
Season
daily 4 July–30 August 2026, 10.00–16.00 (last lift 15.50), also 27–28 June
Lift pass
day pass €22.50 (€26 with the cable car from the village), season pass €231 (2026)
Level
all levels
Hire
hire in Saint-Lary village (shops and prices not given by Altiservice)

Saint-Lary is one of the most established bike parks in the French Pyrenees. The trails lie above Pla d'Adet between 1,700 and 2,215 m, with views over the Vallée d'Aure and towards Néouvielle. The range runs from the Family Rider family trail to the technical K-Bourre and Razorback. The cable car from the village means you can stay down in Saint-Lary and ride up.

Highlights

  • Cable car straight from the village
  • Woodstock, 4.4 km long
  • Beginners' zone and family trail

Getting here

Getting here
Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 1 h 15 min, Toulouse (TLS) approx. 1 h 45 min. Train to Lannemezan, bus to Saint-Lary.
Accommodation
Saint-Lary-Soulan, Vielle-Aure or Pla d'Adet.
TipsA freeride or DH bike, helmet, gloves and knee pads are compulsory. Children under 12 must ride with an adult.
