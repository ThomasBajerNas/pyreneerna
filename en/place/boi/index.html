Vall de Boí

Romanesque churches – World Heritage

Nine Romanesque churches from the 11th–12th centuries; the best is Sant Climent de Taüll. At the head of the valley lies the spa resort of Caldes de Boí. Reached from Luchon via Vielha.

UNESCO World Heritage

Catalan Romanesque Churches of the Vall de Boí

World Heritage since
2000

Nine small Romanesque churches from the 11th and 12th centuries in a mountain valley, with slender Lombard-style bell towers. The murals from Sant Climent de Taüll are now in the MNAC museum in Barcelona, and in the church they are shown by light projection.

Visit: Buy a combined ticket at the Centre del Romànic in Erill la Vall. Climb the bell towers in Taüll and Erill la Vall for the view.
