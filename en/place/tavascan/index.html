Tavascan (Pleta del Prat)

Vall de Cardós (Lladorre), Pallars Sobirà, Lleida, Catalonia

Tavascan is a small resort at the top of the Vall de Cardós. The alpine area at Pleta del Prat had one chairlift and a few short pistes. The alpine section has been closed since March 2025, as the municipality of Lladorre cannot afford to run it. The Generalitat has set aside money for renovations and asked FGC to prepare a takeover, but at the end of August 2026 no decision had been made. The cross-country trails and snowshoe routes are open, however, and are part of Tot Nòrdic.

Tavascan is a small municipally owned resort at the top of the Vall de Cardós. In the 2025/26 season it opened only for cross-country skiing, ski touring and snowshoeing, as the chairlift did not pass its inspection. The base is the Refugi de la Pleta del Prat at 1,720 m, which is being extended. Quiet, wild and authentic.

Highlights

  • Refugi de la Pleta del Prat
  • Good base for ski touring
  • Secluded valley in the Alt Pirineu
