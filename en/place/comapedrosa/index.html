Comapedrosa

Andorra's highest peak, 2 942 m

Andorra's highest peak lies in the Comapedrosa natural park above Arinsal. A long but straightforward hike, with views towards Pica d'Estats and Andorra's valleys.

Andorra's highest peak lies in the Comapedrosa natural park. On a clear day you can see Pica d'Estats and Montcalm to the east.

How to get up – the normal route step by step

  1. From Prats Sobirans steeply up along the stream to Refugi de Comapedrosa.
  2. On past the Estany Negre lake and up the scree slope below the summit.
  3. The last stretch on gravel and stones to the summit. Descend the same way.
SafetyNo technical difficulty, but 1 400 metres of ascent and scree near the summit.
