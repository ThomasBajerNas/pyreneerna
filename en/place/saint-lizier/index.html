Saint-Lizier

A bishop's town with two cathedrals

A small medieval town on a hill above Saint-Girons with a Romanesque cathedral and cloister, a bishop's palace and an 18th-century pharmacy. Part of the pilgrim route to Santiago.

UNESCO World Heritage

Routes of Santiago de Compostela in France

World Heritage since
1998
This site
The cathedral, the cloister and the bishop's palace

71 churches, abbeys, bridges and hospices along the four French pilgrim routes to Santiago de Compostela, plus seven sections of the trail itself. Within the guide's area these include Saint-Sernin in Toulouse, the cathedrals of Saint-Bertrand-de-Comminges, Saint-Lizier and Oloron, the Porte Saint-Jacques in Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port and the abbeys of Moissac, Conques and Saint-Guilhem-le-Désert.

Visit: Walk a day stage on the GR 65 or GR 653 – the scallop shell shows the way. Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port is the starting point for most people walking the Camino Francés over the Pyrenees.
