Le Chioula

Pays d'Ax, Ariège

Chioula lies on a ridge above Ax-les-Thermes, about 15 minutes by car from the town. The trails run through forest and over ridges with views over more than 100 km of the Pyrenees. One loop passes the ruins of the Cathar castle of Montaillou. The staffed Refuge du Chioula lies in the middle of the trail network.
