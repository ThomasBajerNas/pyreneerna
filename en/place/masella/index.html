Masella

Cerdanya (Alp and Das), Girona, Catalonia

Masella is a privately owned resort on the north slope of Tosa d'Alp in the Cerdanya. The forest pistes give good shelter and hold the snow well. Together with La Molina, Masella forms the Alp 2500 ski area, one of the largest in the Pyrenees. Masella has Spain's largest night-skiing area, with about 10 km of floodlit pistes, and a large share of artificial snow.
