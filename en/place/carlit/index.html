Pic Carlit and Les Bouillouses

The highest summit in the Pyrénées-Orientales

Pic Carlit (2 921 m) is the highest summit in the Pyrénées-Orientales. The route up starts from the dam at the Lac des Bouillouses and passes a string of lakes.

Pic Carlit is the highest peak in the Pyrénées-Orientales and towers over the plateau of twelve lakes at Les Bouillouses.

How to get up – the normal route step by step

  1. Past a string of lakes up towards the east side of the peak.
  2. The last stretch is a steep rocky path where you use your hands.
  3. Down the same way or round via the twelve lakes.
SafetyExposed near the summit. Avoid in thunderstorms.
