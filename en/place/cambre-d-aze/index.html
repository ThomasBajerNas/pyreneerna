Cambre d'Aze

Eyne and Saint-Pierre-dels-Forcats, Cerdagne, Pyrénées-Orientales

Cambre d'Aze lies in a glacial cirque below the mountain of the same name and has two entry points: the villages of Eyne and Saint-Pierre-dels-Forcats. The pistes run through pine forest that shelters them from the wind, and the views stretch over the Cerdagne and Capcir towards Spain. Next door is the Vallée d'Eyne nature reserve. The resort also has a 35 m high frozen waterfall for ice climbing.
