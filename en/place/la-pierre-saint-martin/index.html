La Pierre Saint-Martin

Vallée de Barétous (Arette), Pyrénées-Atlantiques

The westernmost major ski resort in the Pyrenees lies in a pine forest on the limestone plateau above the Barétous valley, right on the Spanish border. The village is car-free, with résidences and chalets right by the slopes. Around 70% of the area is green and blue pistes, so families feel at home here, and from Pic d'Arlas you can see both the Basque mountains and the Atlantic on a clear day. The resort opened in 1962. Underground lies one of the world's deepest cave systems, the Gouffre de la Pierre Saint-Martin, and in 2015 Chris Froome won the Tour de France stage that finished here.

Getting here

Flights
Pau (PUF) approx. 85 km, approx. 1 h 30 min · Biarritz (BIQ) approx. 140 km, approx. 2 h · Pamplona (PNA) approx. 110 km, approx. 2 h via Isaba (the pass road may close in snowfall)
Train
Oloron-Sainte-Marie approx. 40 km; Pau (TGV) approx. 75 km. Regional buses from Pau and Oloron during the season
Bus
Regional buses (Nouvelle-Aquitaine) from Pau and Oloron-Sainte-Marie during the season; ski buses from Pau, Oloron, Arette, Bayonne, San Sebastián and Pamplona, some with the lift pass included
Car
A64 to Pau/Soumoulou, then Oloron-Sainte-Marie and Arette, with the last stretch on the D132 up towards the pass. Winter tyres or chains/snow socks in the car are compulsory 1 Nov–31 March. Large free car parks in the village

Accommodation

Accommodation
