Bike Park La Molina

Cerdanya (Alp), Girona, Catalonia

Trails
13 routes: 7–8 downhill trails (incl. Guineu 6.1 km, Catalunya, Comella DH), an 11 km XC loop and Bike Park Kids
Lifts
the Cadí-Moixeró gondola and the Cap de Comella chairlift
Disciplines
downhill, enduro, flow, XC, e-MTB, kids' park
Season
daily 20 June–6 September 2026, then weekends in September and 2–4 October; 10.00–18.00
Lift pass
adult day pass €31, children up to 11 €26, afternoon €28, single ride €21
Level
all levels
Hire
no hire mentioned by the resort; adapted bikes are available via the Adapted Sports Center

La Molina is Spain's oldest ski resort and one of the largest bike parks in the Pyrenees. The trails start at up to 2,340 m and offer a big vertical drop, such as Guineu with almost 680 m of descent. Major races are held here regularly, including the 2025 European Downhill Championships. The park can be reached by train all the way from Barcelona.

Highlights

  • Guineu, 6.1 km and approx. 680 m of descent
  • 2025 European Downhill Championships
  • Train from Barcelona

Getting here

Getting here
Barcelona (BCN) approx. 2 h, Girona (GRO) approx. 2 h. R3 train to La Molina station, then bus.
Accommodation
La Molina, Alp, Puigcerdà or Bellver de Cerdanya.
TipsBuy a season pass before 30 June for a lower price. The last lift runs at 17.45.
