Lacs d'Ayous

Pic du Midi d'Ossau in the mirror

Three lakes below the distinctive volcanic peak of the Pic du Midi d'Ossau, with the Refuge d'Ayous mountain hut by the Lac Gentau.

Day hike

Starting point
Lac de Bious-Artigues, Ossau valley
Distance
approx. 12 km
Elevation gain
700 hm
Time
4–5 h
Level
Moderate
Season
June–October

Circular hike up to the lakes and the Refuge d'Ayous, where the Pic du Midi d'Ossau is reflected in the Lac Gentau – one of the most photographed views in the Pyrenees. Back via the Lac Roumassot.
