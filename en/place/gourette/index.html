Gourette

Vallée d'Ossau (Eaux-Bonnes), Pyrénées-Atlantiques

Gourette is regarded as the oldest ski resort in the Pyrenees: Henri Sallenave was skiing here as early as 1903, and in 1908 the first international ski competition in the Pyrenees was held here. The resort sits in a dramatic cirque below Pic de Ger (2,613 m) and has a vertical drop of over 1,000 m, unusually large for the Pyrenees. Its north-facing aspect and proximity to the Atlantic often bring generous amounts of snow. Nearly half the pistes are red or black, but the beginners' area on the Bézou plateau was renewed in 2024.

Getting here

Flights
Pau (PUF) approx. 60 km, approx. 1 h 05 min · Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 75 km, approx. 1 h 20 min · Biarritz (BIQ) approx. 160 km, approx. 2 h
Train
Pau (TGV), then regional bus via Laruns to Gourette
Bus
Regional buses (Nouvelle-Aquitaine) Pau–Laruns–Gourette; ski bus Pau–Gourette (approx. €36 return incl. lift pass) and from Bayonne and San Sebastián at weekends and during school holidays
Car
A64 to Pau, N134 to Laruns and then the D918 via Eaux-Bonnes. Winter tyres or chains/snow socks in the car are compulsory 1 Nov–31 March. Large free car park du Ley below the village with a free gondola up (under 3 min); paid parking in the centre. The Col d'Aubisque is closed in winter
