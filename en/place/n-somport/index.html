Espace nordique du Somport

Vallée d'Aspe, Pyrénées-Atlantiques

Somport lies right at the pass between France and Spain, in the core zone of the Parc national des Pyrénées. The trails run through open high-mountain terrain at about 1,600 m and are groomed for both classic and skating. Beyond the pass, Candanchú's own Nordic loops take over on the Spanish side. After your outing, the Le Somport 1600 restaurant and a jacuzzi with views over the Aspe valley await.
