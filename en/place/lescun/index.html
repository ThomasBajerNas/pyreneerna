Cirque de Lescun

Limestone peaks in the Aspe valley

The mountain village of Lescun sits below a ring of jagged limestone peaks and is one of the most beautiful spots on the GR 10.

From the village through pastures up to the Lac de Lhurs below the Table des Trois Rois. Part of the way follows the GR 10.
