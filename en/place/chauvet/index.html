Chauvet Cave and Pont d'Arc

36,000-year-old cave art

The replica Grotte Chauvet 2 shows the world's oldest known cave paintings. Next to it the natural arch of Pont d'Arc spans the River Ardèche – popular for canoeing.

UNESCO World Heritage

Decorated Cave of Pont d’Arc (Chauvet Cave), Ardèche

World Heritage since
2014

The world's oldest known figurative cave paintings, around 36,000 years old: lions, rhinoceroses, mammoths and horses painted in charcoal. The cave was discovered in 1994 and is closed to the public.

Visit: The replica Grotte Chauvet 2 in Vallon-Pont-d’Arc recreates the cave at full scale. Combine it with the natural arch of Pont d’Arc over the River Ardèche.
