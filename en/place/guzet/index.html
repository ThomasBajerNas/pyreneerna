Guzet

Ustou, Couserans, Ariège

Guzet is a charming forest resort in the Couserans where wooden chalets are scattered among the spruces, with no high-rise buildings. The ski area has three sectors: steep Freychet for those who want a challenge, Guzet in the middle and gentle Prat Mataou for beginners. From the top you can see Mont Valier, the “lord” of the Couserans. Guzet often gets a lot of snow thanks to its position facing the Atlantic and has one of the oldest reputations in the Pyrenees for fine powder.

Highlights

  • The Guzet-Express rail toboggan run: 1.5 km and a 357 m drop
  • Views of Mont Valier (2,838 m)
  • Piste-basher ride up to the Chalet Beauregard restaurant
  • Speedriding and a protected freeride area

Getting here

Flights
Toulouse-Blagnac (TLS) approx. 150 km, approx. 2 h 15 min · Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 160 km, approx. 2 h 15 min
Car
From Saint-Girons take the D618/D3 towards Oust, then the D8 via Ustou up to the resort (approx. 50 min from Saint-Girons). Alternative route via Aulus-les-Bains. Winter tyres or chains needed.
