Llanos del Hospital

Valle de Benasque, Huesca (Aragon)

Llanos del Hospital lies at 1,750 m at the far end of the Benasque valley, in the Parque Natural Posets-Maladeta below Aneto. The trails follow the broad valley floor towards La Besurta and are groomed daily. The snow is usually reliable. A hotel with a spa is right at the trailhead.
