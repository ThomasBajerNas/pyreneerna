Bordeaux

Port of the Moon and capital of wine

An 18th-century city of pale limestone along the Garonne, with the Cité du Vin wine museum. A good starting point for Saint-Émilion and for the Canal des Deux Mers cycle route along the Garonne towards Toulouse.

UNESCO World Heritage

Bordeaux, Port of the Moon

World Heritage since
2007

The port city on the crescent-shaped bend of the Garonne, with a harmonious 18th-century city of pale limestone along the quays – the Place de la Bourse, the Grand Théâtre and kilometres of façades.

Visit: The Miroir d’eau in front of the Place de la Bourse is the world's largest reflecting pool – go there at dusk.
