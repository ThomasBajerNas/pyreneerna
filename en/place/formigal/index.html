Formigal

Valle de Tena (Sallent de Gállego), Huesca, Aragon

Formigal is the largest ski resort in Aragon and the largest part of Aramón's Formigal-Panticosa ski area. The resort lies at the Formigal development, founded in 1964 above Sallent de Gállego. The slopes spread across four valleys: Sextas, Sarrios, Anayet and Portalet on the French border. There are long, wide red pistes and plenty of off-piste. Formigal is also known for its après-ski. Official figures are only given for Formigal-Panticosa as a whole (182 km, 147 pistes, 38 lifts). The figures here for Formigal alone come from Skiresort.info.

2026/27: from €63 adult/day at the ticket office and from €53.50 online. Children and seniors from €50 (€42.90 online). Half-day from 13.00 from €51.50. The pass is also valid at Panticosa.

all levels, plenty for intermediate and advanced skiers
Snowmaking
Formigal-Panticosa has approx. 800 snow cannons, covering approx. 47 km of pistes
Snow park
Funny Track (fun slope for children) and special pistes. The Petrosos Park is in the Panticosa sector.

Highlights

  • Four valleys: Sextas, Sarrios, Anayet and Portalet
  • Views of the Pic du Midi d'Ossau from Portalet
  • Lively après-ski
  • The same pass is valid at Panticosa

Getting here

Flights
Pau-Pyrénées (PUF) approx. 85 km, approx. 1 h 30 min via the Col du Pourtalet (may close in snowfall) · Zaragoza (ZAZ) approx. 170 km, approx. 2 h · Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 115 km, approx. 1 h 45 min
Train
Sabiñánigo (Renfe, Zaragoza–Huesca–Jaca–Canfranc line) approx. 40 km, with a bus onwards
Bus
Regional buses Huesca/Jaca–Sabiñánigo–Valle de Tena–Formigal (Alosa) and ski buses in the Valle de Tena during the season
Car
A-23 to Sabiñánigo, then the A-136 through the Valle de Tena via Biescas and Sallent de Gállego to Formigal. From France via the Col du Pourtalet (D934/A-136). There are four access points with free parking: Sextas (1,500 m), Sarrios (1,800 m), Anayet (1,740 m) and Portalet. Paid parking can also be booked. Chains are often needed.

Accommodation

Accommodation
Wide choice in the Formigal development: hotels and flats near the lifts at Sextas, several ski-in/ski-out
Places to stay nearby
Sallent de Gállego (approx. 5 min), Escarrilla and Tramacastilla (approx. 10 min), Biescas (approx. 25 min), Panticosa (approx. 20 min)
TipsBook your lift pass online – it is 10–15% cheaper than at the ticket office. Start at Anayet or Portalet when there are queues at Sextas. The pass is also valid at Panticosa, which is a good fallback in strong winds.
