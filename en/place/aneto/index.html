Aneto

The highest peak in the Pyrenees, 3 404 m

The roof of the Pyrenees rises in the Maladeta massif above Benasque in Aragon. The summit is reached across the Aneto glacier and the narrow rock ridge of Pont de Mahomet. From Plan de la Besurta and the La Renclusa hut you can see the whole massif.

Pont de Mahomet, the last narrow ridge before the summit, was named by Franqueville after a Muslim legend that the path to paradise is as narrow as the edge of a sabre. The glacier has shrunk from over 100 hectares in 1981 to around 30 hectares in 2025, and scientists believe the Pyrenean glaciers could be gone within a decade.

How to get up – the normal route step by step

  1. Day 1: from Plan de la Besurta, a good hour up to La Renclusa. Early dinner and early to bed.
  2. Day 2: set off before dawn and climb through the boulder fields to Portillón Superior (approx. 2 870 m), the notch in the ridge.
  3. Put on crampons and rope up, then cross the Aneto glacier towards Collado de Coronas. The crevasses are hard to see when there is snow.
  4. A steep snow slope leads up to the fore-summit. From there you cross Pont de Mahomet – an exposed rock ridge just under 30 metres long (II) – to the summit cross.
  5. Descend the same way. Be back on the glacier before midday, when the snow turns slushy and the thunderstorms arrive.
SafetyGlacier route: rope, harness, crampons, ice axe and helmet. In late summer the glacier is bare ice with open crevasses, and rockfall is common at Portillón. Accidents happen every year – go with a mountain guide if you lack glacier experience.

Other routes up

From the south a route runs from the Vallibierna valley via the Coronas lakes, with less glacier but a longer approach.
