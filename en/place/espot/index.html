Espot Esquí

Pallars Sobirà, Lleida, Catalonia

Espot Esquí opened in 1967 and lies above the village of Espot, at the gateway to the Aigüestortes i Estany de Sant Maurici National Park. The resort is small and personal, with pistes close to the forest and views of Els Encantats. Besides the pistes there are 26 km of off-piste and ski-touring routes. It is run by FGC, like its neighbour Port Ainé, and has Spain's first floating solar park at a ski resort.

Getting here

Flights
Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell (LEU) approx. 95 km, approx. 1 h 30 min · Lleida-Alguaire (ILD) approx. 175 km, approx. 2 h 15 min · Barcelona (BCN) approx. 245 km, approx. 3 h 15 min
Bus
ALSA buses Barcelona–Sort–Esterri d'Àneu stop at the turn-off for Espot. No bus up to the slopes.
Car
C-13 through the Pallars Sobirà past Sort and Llavorsí, then the LV-5004 to Espot and the road up to Super Espot (approx. 7 km). Free parking at La Roca. Chains may be needed.
