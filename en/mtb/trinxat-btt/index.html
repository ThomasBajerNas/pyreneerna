Mountain biking

Trinxat BTT

Loop Alt Urgell – Cerdanya (booking in Alàs)

Route
Loop Alt Urgell – Cerdanya (booking in Alàs)
Distance
approx. 298 km
Elevation gain
approx. 7,600 m of climbing
Days
5 days (2–3 in the extreme version)

Trinxat BTT is a loop linking Alt Urgell and Cerdanya, passing close to Pallars Sobirà and Andorra. Many sections are on gravel roads at around 2,000 m with wide views over the Segre valley. The name comes from Cerdanya's classic dish trinxat (cabbage, potato and pork).

TipsSeason May–October depending on snow. Packages with accommodation and luggage transfers are booked through the organiser.
