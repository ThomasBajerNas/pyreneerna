Espacio BTT Pirineos Alto Gállego

Alto Gállego and Valle de Tena, Huesca (Aragon)

Trails
over 500 km of signposted routes, incl. descent trails (DH1, DH2, DH14)
Disciplines
enduro, all mountain, XC, e-MTB
Season
approx. May–October; higher routes July–September
Cost
free trails
Level
intermediate and advanced

Around Biescas and Sabiñánigo there is a large signposted network of routes in the Gállego valley and the Valle de Tena. From the Piedrafita lake a legendary descent of up to 2,500 m in total awaits those with the stamina. Local guides run shuttles and lead rides between the villages. The proximity of Panticosa and Formigal means you can combine it with lift-served riding.

Highlights

  • Long descent from Piedrafita in the Valle de Tena
  • Biescas as a base
  • Close to Panticosa and Formigal

Getting here

Getting here
Zaragoza approx. 2 h by car, Pau approx. 1 h 45 min over the Col du Pourtalet.
Accommodation
Biescas, Sabiñánigo, Panticosa, Sallent de Gállego
TipsNot all trails are on the official map – a local guide knows the best ones. The Valle de Tena borders high mountains where the weather can change quickly.
