Naturland – La Rabassa

Sant Julià de Lòria, Andorra

Naturland is Andorra's only cross-country area, in the forest above Sant Julià de Lòria. The trails are at Cota 2000 and run through pine forest and across open pastures. Further down, at Cota 1600, there is an adventure centre with one of Europe's longest toboggan runs. A good complement to alpine skiing in Andorra.
