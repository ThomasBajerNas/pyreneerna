Bike Park Peyragudes – Loudenvielle

Vallée du Louron, Hautes-Pyrénées/Haute-Garonne

Trails
downhill, enduro and flow trails in the Loudenvielle, Peyresourde, Altiport and Agudes sectors; 6 new trails added in 2026, incl. AP4 Airline (black), Blue Wave Agudes (blue, 3.5 km) and a dual slalom course; 13 km FlowTrail (total number not given)
Lifts
the Skyvall gondola from Loudenvielle, the Privilège chairlift and the Téléluge du Lac (Agudes)
Disciplines
downhill, enduro, flow, jump line, dual slalom, e-MTB
Season
Skyvall daily 4 July–30 August 2026 and at weekends in May, June, September and October
Lift pass
bike day pass €28 (gondola or drag lift) or €31 (both), adults aged 15–69; discount for ages 5–7 and 70+ (2026)
Level
all levels
Hire
several shops in Loudenvielle and at Skyvall (prices not given)

Loudenvielle and Peyragudes are the big gravity hub of the Pyrenees. The Skyvall gondola takes you from the valley floor up to the resort in under ten minutes. The downhill World Cup was held here in May 2026 during the Pyrénées Bike Festival. With 13 km of FlowTrail, new jump lines and long enduro trails, there is something for everyone.

Highlights

  • UCI downhill World Cup 28–31 May 2026
  • 13 km FlowTrail, one of the longest in the Pyrenees
  • New dual slalom at Skyvall

Getting here

Getting here
Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 1 h 15 min, Toulouse (TLS) approx. 1 h 50 min. Train to Lannemezan or Luchon, then bus.
Accommodation
Loudenvielle (with the Balnéa spa), Peyresourde, Les Agudes or Luchon.
TipsBook your pass online and collect it from the machine at Skyvall. Enduro shuttles (Loudenvielle, Col d'Azet, Val Louron) are booked via Trailow.com the day before, €35/day.
