Centre BTT Valls d'Àneu

Pallars Sobirà, Lleida (Catalonia)

Trails
30 routes, approx. 1,205 km: approx. 21 km very easy, 193 km intermediate, 178 km difficult, 815 km very difficult
Disciplines
XC, all mountain, marathon, e-MTB
Season
approx. June–October
Cost
free trails
Level
intermediate and advanced

Valls d'Àneu has one of the largest signposted networks in the Catalan Pyrenees, with Esterri d'Àneu as its base. The valleys border the Alt Pirineu natural park and Aigüestortes national park, and the routes follow old tracks between small stone villages. Most of the kilometres are long, physically demanding rides.

Highlights

  • Over 1,200 km of routes
  • Borders Aigüestortes national park
  • Romanesque churches and stone villages

TipsCycling is not permitted on the paths in Aigüestortes national park – follow the waymarked routes. Very difficult routes are often long rather than technical; allow full days.
