Les Monts d'Olmes

Montferrier, Pays d'Olmes, Ariège

Les Monts d'Olmes lies in the “Cathar Pyrenees” above Lavelanet, near the castle of Montségur. It is a family resort with a mix of green and red pistes and a calm atmosphere close to nature. The mogul skier Perrine Laffont grew up here – she won Olympic gold in 2018 and has given her name to the mogul run below the resort.

Foix (liO train from Toulouse). Skirail 2025/26: train + bus from Foix + day pass for €42, on certain Saturdays in January–February.
Bus
Skirail bus Foix–Monts d'Olmes (approx. 1 h) on the days the package is sold
Car
From Lavelanet take the D909 via Montferrier up to the resort (approx. 20 km). Approx. 50 min from Foix. Winter tyres or chains needed.
