San Sebastián

A very beautiful city

From the 2014 guide – ThomasA very beautiful city.

A Basque coastal city on La Concha bay with one of Europe's finest city beaches. The old town (Parte Vieja) is famous for pintxos – small bites at the bar. Walk up Monte Urgull or take the funicular up Monte Igueldo for the view over the bay.
