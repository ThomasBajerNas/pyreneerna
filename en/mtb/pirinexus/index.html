Pirinexus (gravel/cycle touring)

Loop Girona – Olot – Céret – L'Escala – Girona

Route
Loop Girona – Olot – Céret – L'Escala – Girona
Distance
approx. 353 km
Days
4–6 days

Pirinexus is a cross-border loop between Catalonia and Roussillon that largely follows old railway lines (vías verdes), minor roads and gravel roads. The highest point is the Col d'Ares at approx. 1,500 m, and the loop passes Girona, the volcanic landscape around Olot, Prats-de-Mollo, Céret and the Costa Brava. It suits a gravel, touring or hybrid bike better than a full-suspension MTB.

TipsClockwise is most common. Between Prats-de-Mollo and Arles-sur-Tech you share the road with motor traffic.
