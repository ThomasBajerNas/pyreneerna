Espace VTT-FFC Aude en Pyrénées

Pyrénées Audoises (Quillan), Aude

Trails
40 loops, approx. 890 km (2019 figure); linked with neighbouring areas it comes to almost 2,000 km
Disciplines
XC, all mountain, enduro, e-MTB
Season
approx. March–November; higher parts May–October
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels

Around Quillan and the gorges of the Aude valley lies one of the largest waymarked MTB networks in the Pyrenees. The landscape alternates between beech forests, limestone plateaus and ravines such as the Gorges de Saint-Georges, and several Cathar castles are within reach. The Mediterranean climate means the season starts early and ends late.

Highlights

  • One of the largest FFC networks in the Pyrenees
  • Gorges and Cathar castles
  • Long season
