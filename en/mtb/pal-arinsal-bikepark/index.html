Bike Park Pal Arinsal (Vallnord, La Massana)

La Massana, Andorra

Pal Arinsal, formerly Vallnord Bike Park, is one of Europe's best-known bike parks and hosts the MTB World Cup almost every year. The gondola runs straight from La Massana, and the trails reach all the way down to the village. With over 60 km of trails, there is everything from family flow trails to the World Cup downhill course. Commencal is based here, and the atmosphere is international.

Highlights

  • UCI MTB World Cup 9–12 July 2026
  • The Best World DHI 2019 World Cup course
  • Over 60 km of trails from La Massana

Getting here

Getting here
Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell (LEU) approx. 35 min, Barcelona (BCN) approx. 2 h 45 min, Toulouse (TLS) approx. 3 h. Bus from Barcelona to Andorra.
Accommodation
La Massana, Arinsal, Erts or Andorra la Vella.
TipsBuy your pass in advance online or at the ticket office by the gondola. Day passes from the machines are sold until 14.30. Insurance is not included.
