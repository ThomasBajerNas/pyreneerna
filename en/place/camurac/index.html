Camurac

Pays de Sault, Aude

Camurac is the only alpine resort in the Aude and lies at the Col du Teil on the high Pays de Sault plateau. It is a small resort with 11 pistes and four drag lifts plus a rope tow in the beginners' area. From here you look towards Saint-Barthélemy and Soularac (2,348 m). Perfect for a quiet day on the slopes, and the area is lovely for snowshoeing.
