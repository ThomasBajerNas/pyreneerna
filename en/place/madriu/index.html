Madriu-Perafita-Claror Valley

Andorra's roadless wilderness valley

A glacial valley with lakes, dry-stone walls, summer farms and old forges, just an hour's walk from Andorra la Vella. Trails from Escaldes-Engordany.

Along the old paved path into the World Heritage valley to the Refugi de Fontverd and the summer pastures. If you have two days, cross over to Perafita.

UNESCO World Heritage

Madriu-Perafita-Claror Valley

World Heritage since
2004 (extended 2006)

A roadless glacial valley in Andorra, where shepherds, forestry and iron-making have shaped the landscape for a thousand years. It covers almost a tenth of the country.

Visit: Hiking trails start in Escaldes-Engordany. There are unstaffed huts (refugis) for overnight stays.
