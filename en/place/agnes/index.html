Col d'Agnes

A high-mountain road between Lers and Aulus

Cycling climb

Height
1,570 m

A narrow, open road across the bare mountainside with views towards the border ridge and Mont Valier to the west. Almost no cars. The descent ends in Aulus-les-Bains – a soak in the thermal baths or a walk to the Cascade d'Ars afterwards.

Tour de FranceIncluded in 2017 together with the Col de Latrape and the Mur de Péguère on the Saint-Girons – Foix stage.
