Espace nordique de La Pierre Saint-Martin (Le Braca)

Vallée de Barétous, Pyrénées-Atlantiques

The cross-country area lies about 2 km below the alpine resort of La Pierre Saint-Martin, in the Forêt du Braca with its centuries-old mountain pines. The forest is a protected natural area (Espace Naturel Sensible). The trails wind between trees and clearings, and there are unusually many other winter activities. A good choice if the family wants to mix cross-country skiing with dog sledding or fat biking.

Highlights

  • Dog sledding and fat biking on their own trails

  • Shuttle bus from the alpine resort

Getting here

Flights
Pau-Pyrénées (PUF) approx. 75–90 km, approx. 1 h 30 min · Biarritz (BIQ) approx. 145 km, approx. 2 h 15 min · Pamplona (PNA) approx. 120 km, approx. 2 h
Train
Oloron-Sainte-Marie, approx. 40 km – ski bus from there
Bus
Ski bus from Oloron-Sainte-Marie; shuttle bus from La Pierre Saint-Martin to the cross-country area €2 return (free with a trail pass)
Car
From Oloron via Arette and the D132 (23 km from Arette). Coach parking available. Snow chains or winter tyres are often required.
