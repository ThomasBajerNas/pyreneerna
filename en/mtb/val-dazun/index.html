VTT Val d'Azun

Val d'Azun (Arrens-Marsous), Hautes-Pyrénées

Trails
at least 14 numbered VTT loops (e.g. no. 14 Soulor–Couraduque, 13.5 km, easy); total unknown
Disciplines
XC, all mountain, e-MTB
Season
approx. June–October
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels

The Val d'Azun is a green pastoral valley between Argelès-Gazost and the Col du Soulor, at the gateway to the Parc National des Pyrénées. The numbered loops run through forests and meadows and past lakes such as the Lac de Soum, and several start from the Col du Soulor and the Col de Couraduque. Here the focus is on peace, grazing animals and views rather than speed.

Highlights

  • Col du Soulor and Couraduque
  • Lac de Soum
  • Quiet valley by the national park
