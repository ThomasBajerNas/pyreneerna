Port del Comte

Solsonès (La Coma i la Pedra), Lleida, Catalonia

Port del Comte opened in 1973 and lies in the mountains above the Solsonès. It is the alpine resort closest to Barcelona and is popular for day trips. The area spreads over several sectors, such as El Querol and Estivella, with wide pistes and broad views over Catalonia all the way to Montserrat. Ahead of 2025/26 the entire cable of the Sucre chairlift was replaced and more than 725 m of windbreaks were put up.
