Minerve

The hill village where the first great Cathar pyre burned

The village sits on a rocky spur between two gorges in the Minervois. The River Cesse has carved natural bridges in the limestone below the village. The home of the Minervois wines.

The history of the Cathars

In July 1210 Minerve fell after a seven-week siege. The Malvoisine catapult had destroyed the steps down to the well. 140 Perfects who refused to renounce their faith were burned – the first great Cathar pyre. A memorial with a dove stands in the village.
