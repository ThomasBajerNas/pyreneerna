Mont-Louis

Vauban's citadel at 1,600 metres

France's highest fortified town, built in 1679–1681 by Vauban to guard the border with Spain. The citadel is still used by the army. It is also home to the world's first solar furnace, from 1949, which you can visit.

UNESCO World Heritage

Fortifications of Vauban

World Heritage since
2008
This site
The citadel and the walled town

Twelve fortifications along France's borders designed by the military engineer Vauban for Louis XIV. In the Pyrenees they include the walled town of Villefranche-de-Conflent with Fort Libéria and the citadel town of Mont-Louis, France's highest fortified town.

Visit: Villefranche and Mont-Louis lie on the line of the yellow train, the Train Jaune – take the train between them.
