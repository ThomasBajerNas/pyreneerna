Baqueira Beret BTT

Val d'Aran (Naut Aran), Lleida, Catalonia

Trails
11 BTT trails from family loops to technical descents, plus dirt jump
Lifts
the Bosque and Clòt der Os chairlifts
Disciplines
enduro, downhill riding, e-MTB, dirt jump
Season
from 1 July 2026; Bosque until 6 September, Clòt der Os until 30 August, 10.00–18.00
Lift pass
bike pass adult €30, child €20; 2 days adult €56 (2026)
Level
all levels

Baqueira is Spain's best-known ski resort and in summer a quiet base for enduro in the Val d'Aran. Two chairlifts carry bikes up to trails through forest and alpine meadows. The trails link up with waymarked enduro trails in the valley. It is not a dedicated bike park, but a fine destination for anyone who wants to combine lifts and nature.

Highlights

  • Two bike-carrying chairlifts
  • 11 trails for all levels
  • The green valleys of the Val d'Aran
