The Massane Forest

Primeval beech forest above the Mediterranean

One of Europe's southernmost primeval beech forests, in the Albères mountains between Argelès and the Spanish border. Old, hollow trees are left standing to decay in peace, and scientists have been studying the forest for more than half a century.

UNESCO World Heritage

Ancient and Primeval Beech Forests of the Carpathians and Other Regions of Europe

World Heritage since
2007 (Massane 2021)

The Massane Forest

A European series of untouched beech forests. The Massane Forest in the Albères mountains above Argelès-sur-Mer is one of the French components: one of the southernmost primeval beech forests in Europe, close to the Mediterranean.

Visit: The forest is a strict nature reserve. Hike there from the Col de l’Ouillat or Argelès and stay on the paths.
