Goulier Neige

Vicdessos, Ariège

Goulier calls itself the smallest resort in the Pyrenees and lies in the Vicdessos valley at the foot of Montcalm (3,077 m), the highest peak in Ariège. Green and blue pistes suit small children and beginners, and there are a few safe powder areas for more experienced skiers. The atmosphere is friendly and the prices are low.
