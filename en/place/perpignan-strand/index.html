The Mediterranean beaches near Perpignan

Lovely swimming in the Mediterranean

From the 2014 guide – ThomasThere are loads of beaches. One problem on the Mediterranean, though: parking is scarce.

Canet-en-Roussillon, Sainte-Marie and Saint-Cyprien have long sandy beaches. Arrive before ten or after four to find parking.
