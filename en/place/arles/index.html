Arles

A Roman arena and Van Gogh's town

The amphitheatre from AD 90 is still used for performances. Arles is the gateway to the Camargue with its flamingos and white horses.

UNESCO World Heritage

Arles, Roman and Romanesque Monuments

World Heritage since
1981

The amphitheatre, the ancient theatre, the cryptoporticus, the Baths of Constantine and the Alyscamps necropolis from Roman times, plus the church of Saint-Trophime with its famous medieval portal and cloister.

Visit: A single pass covers all the monuments. Van Gogh painted here in 1888 – seek out the spots around town.
