Espace VTT-FFC Pyrénées Catalanes

Capcir, Haut-Conflent and Haute-Cerdagne, Pyrénées-Orientales

Trails
42 loops, approx. 653 km: 6 green, 14 blue, 17 red, 3 black; plus 7 enduro trails (approx. 62 km, 2 blue, 5 red) and a 127 km village tour
Disciplines
XC, enduro, all mountain, e-MTB
Season
waymarked as open all year round; in practice snow-free approx. June–October
Cost
free trails
Level
all levels
Hire
hire in Les Angles, Font-Romeu and Formiguères; price unknown

One of the largest FFC-waymarked areas in the Pyrenees, on the high plateau around the Col de la Quillane, Les Angles, Formiguères and Font-Romeu. The mountain pine forests and many lakes make for easy but beautiful riding, and seven enduro trails make the area interesting for those who want speed too. The plateau is known for its many hours of sunshine.

Highlights

  • 653 km of FFC-waymarked loops
  • 7 enduro trails
  • Grand Tour des villages, 127 km
