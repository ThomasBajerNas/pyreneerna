Andorra la Vella

Tax-free town

From the 2014 guide – ThomasMammon's temple in the Pyrenees – there are still some bargains to be had. Electronics, tobacco, alcohol and clothes are good value.

Andorra is outside the EU customs union – check the customs allowances for what you take home. Combine the shopping with Caldea in the neighbouring town of Escaldes-Engordany.
