Piau Bike (Piau-Engaly)

Haute vallée d'Aure (Aragnouet), Hautes-Pyrénées

Trails
approx. 17 km of trails in Piau plus approx. 15 km further down towards the Fabian Bike Center
Lifts
the Campbielh chairlift (summer service Sunday–Thursday)
Disciplines
enduro, flow, downhill riding, mountain scooter, dévalkart
Season
Campbielh chairlift 5 July–28 August 2026, Sunday–Thursday 9.00–12.00 and 13.30–15.30
Lift pass
lift pass from €7 on foot; mountain scooter day pass €14.50 (bike price not given)
Level
all levels

Piau-Engaly is the highest French ski resort in the Pyrenees and lies just below the Bielsa tunnel. From the top of the Campbielh chairlift at approx. 2,350 m, trails descend through a stark high-mountain landscape. The long trail continues all the way down towards Fabian and Pont du Moudang, with a shuttle back. Quieter and smaller than the big bike parks.

Highlights

  • Start at approx. 2,350 m
  • Long descent towards the Fabian Bike Center
  • Shuttle bus back from Pont du Moudang

Getting here

Getting here
Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 1 h 30 min, Toulouse (TLS) approx. 2 h 15 min. Train to Lannemezan, then bus.
Accommodation
Piau-Engaly, Saint-Lary-Soulan or Vielle-Aure.
TipsThe chairlift does not run on Fridays and Saturdays. Check with the tourist office (05 62 39 61 69) which bike passes apply before you go.
