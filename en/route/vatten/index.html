Road trip

The waterfall loop

Cauterets → Gavarnie → Troumouse → Luchon · 190 km

Distance
190 km

Two or three days among the biggest waterfalls in the Pyrenees. The Chemin des Cascades and the Pont d'Espagne at Cauterets, Gavarnie's 420-metre fall, Troumouse, and then over the Tourmalet and the Aspin to the Lac d'Oô and the Vallée du Lys near Luchon. Most water in June and early July.
