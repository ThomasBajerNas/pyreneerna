Bike Park La Pierre Saint-Martin

Vallée de Barétous (Arette), Pyrénées-Atlantiques

Trails
5 enduro trails: 2 blue, 2 red, 1 black, plus a beginners' zone by the Oursons magic carpet
Lifts
the Arlas chairlift (and the Oursons magic carpet for the beginners' zone)
Disciplines
enduro, e-MTB, beginners' zone
Season
July–August, 10.00–13.00 and 14.00–18.00 (exact dates for 2026 not given; the chairlift opens in early July)
Lift pass
adult day pass €20 (€17 with the No Souci card)
Level
all levels
Hire
MTB, e-MTB and electric scooter hire at the resort (price not given)

A small, quiet bike park on the karst plateau at the top of Barétous, right on the border with Navarre. The trails are natural enduro trails rather than built jump lines. The landscape of limestone boulders, pines and grazing sheep makes the riding special. Good for anyone who wants to try lift-served enduro without the crowds.

Highlights

  • Limestone landscape on the Spanish border
  • The Arlas chairlift takes you up
  • Beginners' zone for children and novices

Getting here

Getting here
Pau (PUF) approx. 1 h 30 min, Biarritz (BIQ) approx. 2 h, Pamplona approx. 2 h via Isaba. Train to Oloron-Sainte-Marie (approx. 40 km).
Accommodation
The resort village (résidences and apartments), Arette or Oloron-Sainte-Marie.
TipsHelmets are compulsory. Be considerate of grazing animals and close gates. There is a repair station but no large bike shop, so bring spare parts.
