Col d'Aspin

A green col with cows and views of the Pic du Midi

Cycling climb

Height
1,489 m
From
Arreau

Gentler than its neighbours but with the finest view – the Pic du Midi straight to the west. Cows and horses graze freely on the road. The third col of the reverse queen stage.
