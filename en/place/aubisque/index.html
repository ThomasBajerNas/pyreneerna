From the 2014 guide – ThomasBeautiful, Tour de France country. Watch out for the cows on the road!

The first col of the reverse queen stage from Pau. Between the Aubisque and the Col du Soulor runs a narrow ledge road, the Cirque du Litor, with sheer drops. The giant bikes at the col are a classic photo opportunity.
