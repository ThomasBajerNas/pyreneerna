Astún

Valle del Aragón (Jaca), Huesca, Aragon

Astún opened in 1976 and lies in a high valley a few kilometres from Candanchú, near the French border. The resort is more modern and compact than its neighbour, with accommodation and lifts at the same base. The slopes reach up to La Raca and Truchas, and from the summits you can see Anayet and the Midi d'Ossau. There are unusually many red and black runs, and the wide valleys towards Canal Roya and Sarrios are good for carving. Astún is part of 100K together with Candanchú.

Getting here

Flights
Pau-Pyrénées (PUF) approx. 100 km, approx. 1 h 35 min · Zaragoza (ZAZ) approx. 180 km, approx. 2 h 5 min · Lourdes-Tarbes (LDE) approx. 140 km, approx. 2 h 5 min
Train
Canfranc-Estación (Renfe from Zaragoza via Huesca and Jaca) approx. 15 km, with a bus onwards
Bus
Regional buses Jaca–Canfranc–Candanchú/Astún (Alosa). Free shuttle bus between Astún and Candanchú for 100K pass holders.
Car
N-330 from Jaca towards the Somport, then the turn-off up to Astún just before the border (approx. 33 km from Jaca). Parking at the resort base. Winter equipment and chains are often required.

TipsArrive early on a Saturday, as the car park fills quickly at weekends. In strong winds the highest lifts sometimes close. Then it may be better to ski in sheltered Candanchú on the same pass.
