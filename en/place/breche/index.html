Brèche de Roland

The gateway in the mountain wall above Gavarnie

A gap 40 metres wide and 100 metres high in the ridge at an altitude of 2 807 metres. According to legend the knight Roland cut it with his sword Durendal. A classic hike from the Col des Tentes.

Day hike

Starting point
Col des Tentes (2 208 m), above Gavarnie
Distance
approx. 12 km return
Elevation gain
900 hm
Time
6–7 h
Level
Hard
Season
July–September

Up via the Port de Boucharo and the Refuge des Sarradets mountain hut to the breach at 2 807 metres, with views straight down into Spain towards Ordesa. The final stretch crosses a snowfield that can be icy – crampons are often needed in early summer.
