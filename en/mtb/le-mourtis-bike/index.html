Le Mourtis is a small forest resort above the Col de Menté. In summer 2026 the Bambins drag lift takes riders up to easy practice trails. It suits children and beginners who want to learn downhill riding in a calm setting. The resort's new mountain school offers MTB lessons.

Highlights

  • Enduro du Mourtis 18–19 July 2026
  • New MTB school
  • Peaceful forest setting
