Zona Zero Sobrarbe-Pirineo

Sobrarbe, Huesca (Aragon)

Trails
over 1,000 km of natural trails across eight sectors with signposted routes and GPS tracks
Disciplines
enduro, all mountain, XC, e-MTB, shuttle
Season
all year round in the lower parts; best in spring and autumn, summer higher up
Cost
free trails
Level
intermediate and advanced
Hire
several bike shops and guides in Aínsa and Boltaña hire out enduro bikes and e-MTBs and run shuttles; price unknown

Zona Zero is Spain's best-known enduro area, centred on the medieval village of Aínsa. The trails are old paths linking villages, many of them now abandoned, and local enthusiasts have been clearing and signposting them since the early 2000s. The terrain is dry, rocky and playful, with views of Monte Perdido. In 2015 Aínsa became the first Spanish round of the Enduro World Series, with a prologue through the town's alleys.

Highlights

  • 2015 EWS round with a prologue in Aínsa's old town
  • Trails between abandoned villages
  • Rideable almost all year round
