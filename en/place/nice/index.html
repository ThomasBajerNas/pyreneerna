Nice

The Riviera's winter resort

The Promenade des Anglais along the Baie des Anges, the old town and the Matisse and Chagall museums.

UNESCO World Heritage

Nice, Winter Resort Town of the Riviera

World Heritage since
2021

From the mid-18th century the mild winter climate made Nice a resort for Europe's upper classes. The World Heritage site covers the districts that grew up for the winter guests, with the Promenade des Anglais, villas, palace hotels and parks.

Visit: Walk the Promenade des Anglais to the Colline du Château for the view, and on to the old town, Vieux Nice.
