Cirque de Troumouse

Gavarnie's quieter big brother

A huge cirque, bigger than Gavarnie but with fewer visitors. A toll road goes up from Gèdre to about 2 100 metres, and from there it's short walks across the pastures with waterfalls on the surrounding rock walls.

UNESCO World Heritage

Pyrénées – Mont Perdu

World Heritage since
1997 (extended 1999)
This site
Cirque de Troumouse

A cross-border World Heritage site around the summit of Monte Perdido (3,355 m): on the French side the gigantic cirques of Gavarnie, Estaubé and Troumouse, on the Spanish side some of Europe's deepest canyons, Ordesa and Añisclo. It is protected both for its nature and for its ancient pastoral landscape.

Visit: The Cirque de Gavarnie and the Ordesa valley are reached on hiking trails from the villages of Gavarnie and Torla. In summer buses run to Ordesa from Torla – private cars are not allowed then.
