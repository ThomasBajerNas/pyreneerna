Andorra's MTB trails

Andorra's seven parròquies

Trails
over 600 km of trails and descents, dozens of signposted routes; plus over 100 km of e-MTB routes in Grandvalira and the skills park at Engolasters
Disciplines
XC, enduro, all mountain, e-MTB, trials/skills
Season
approx. June–October
Cost
free trails (public routes)
Level
all levels
Hire
hire in several towns; guided rides via e.g. MTB Adventures, Andorra Bike Tracks and VSL Sports

Besides the bike parks at Pal Arinsal and Grandvalira, Andorra has a large network of public, signposted routes in all seven parròquies, from child-friendly rides to trails marked “experts only”. The steep landscape suits enduro, and many routes offer hundreds of metres of singletrack descent down towards the valleys. At Engolasters in Escaldes-Engordany there is a skills park with berms and wooden bridges.

Highlights

  • Over 600 km of trails
  • Skills park at Engolasters
  • Combine with Pal Arinsal and Grandvalira
